## Printing

When the printer is on, it usually auto-connects to the Pawprint Prototyping wifi, and any other computers on the wifi should be able to automatically see the printer.

You can check that the printer is handling connections correctly through HTTP server.  It can be found at the address `dellbaa984.infra.pawprint.space`.

### Drivers
This printer really likes for you to use the Dell drivers
[Windows](https://dl.dell.com/FOLDER01412536M/1/XPS_2155.zip)

## Scanning

Scanning is most easily done by downloading the scanning driver, which can be downloaded here:

* [General Download link](https://www.dell.com/support/product-details/en-us/product/dell-2155cn-cdn/drivers)

Scanning is supported over the wifi. You may need to plug in the printer IP address into the driver to get a working connection, as detecting the printer automatically may fail.

# Safety and Certification

No certifications are required to scan or print.
