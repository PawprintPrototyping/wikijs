# No Sleeping at Pawprint

Sleeping at Pawprint is not [excellent](/excellence).  The hackerspace is a place for hacking on technology and materials, you cannot be hacking if you are asleep.

Sleeping in the space is a violation of lease and insurance terms, and people living in the space reduces its utility for pursuing our mission.

If you are sleeping at the space, anyone else may ask you to wake up and hack, or request that you leave.
