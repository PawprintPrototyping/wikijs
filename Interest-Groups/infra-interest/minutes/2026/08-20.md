## We're setting up a CBRS 4G node deployment!

In 2017, the FCC set up a new [Citizens Band Radio Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citizens_Broadband_Radio_Service) allocation in the 3.5GHz band, which gives us mere mortals access to a legal chunk of the 4G bands on 3GPP bands 48 & 49.  That means anyone can legally run our own 4G/5G tower instead of winging it on an SDR.

We are using this setup as an opportunity to learn more about how 4G networks work, and at the end should have our very own 4G data and telephony stack connected to the Pawprint PBX, authenticated with custom SIM cards.

![open5gs component diagram](https://open5gs.org/open5gs/assets/images/Open5GS_CUPS-01.jpg)
